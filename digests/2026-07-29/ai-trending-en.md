### The "Agent Harness" Ecosystem Matures
`affaan-m/ECC` (+692) and `shareAI-lab/learn-claude-code` (⭐72,504) reveal that the **agent harness**—the middleware layer between LLM and tools—has become a distinct product category. These projects optimize performance (token usage, memory, skills) for coding agents like Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor. The emergence of `iOfficeAI/AionUi` (⭐31,004) as a "cowork app" for multiple CLI agents signals that **multi-agent orchestration UIs** are becoming infrastructure.

This is a strong signal that as agents move from prototypes to production, **governance and sandboxing** will become critical.

### Connection to Recent LLM Releases

The `huggingface/transformers` ecosystem remains dominant (⭐163K), but specialized tools like `minimind` (train 64M models in 2h) suggest a **democratization of LLM training** that parallels the earlier "build your own" movement.

### Vector Databases: Consolidation Phase
